Keeper's notes

  • Creatures walk the shore path to the lighthouse. A leak costs ember.
  • Frost Lanterns slow the march — put them where the path bends.
  • Storm Spires hit hard and their bolts leap. Save them for brutes.
  • A Tide Bell marks foes — the next hit on a marked creature lands +15.
  • The sea brings a different tide each wave: Still Air makes first hits land +50%, Ashfall shortens every tower's reach 8%, Blackout slows the towers in columns 6–10. The forecast is above.
  • The Beacon Flare burns a 3×3 patch for 4s — 30 damage a second, 35% slow — once per wave, for 2 ember. It needs 3 ember left to fire. Aim it where the path is thickest.
  • After each wave, the ember offers a blessing. Choose one; it lasts the run, up to twice each.
  • The Dread Moth splits at two thirds of its health, and its shadow dims the towers at one third.
  • Clearing a wave pays a bonus. Spend it before the next horn.
