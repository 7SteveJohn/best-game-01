Net the Night

Night has swallowed the shore. The lighthouse beam hurts nothing anymore — it catches. You are the Keeper: sweep the light across the dark sea, gather the creatures that crawl toward the ember, and carry them to the furnaces before they struggle free. The Dread Moth fills the whole net, and only a furnace that still holds one of its shell pieces can break it. Hold the light through all eight watches.

    Keeper's notes

    • The beam catches, it does not hurt. A creature inside the cone for a quarter of a second is gathered.
    • The net holds 5 weight: a Wisp or a Shade weighs 1, a Wraith 2, a Brute 3, the Dread Moth 5.
    • Caught creatures struggle free in 3 seconds — Wraiths in under 2. Deliver before they break loose.
    • Let go over a lit furnace to burn the catch: 100 points a creature, 25 more for each extra one in the same delivery.
    • A furnace needs 6 seconds to cool after a burn. Rotate your deliveries.
    • Let go anywhere else and the catch slips free where it was caught — catching never resets its journey.
    • The Dread Moth fills the whole net and must be delivered three times. Each furnace can break only one of its shell pieces.
    • Wisps cross the sea in 10 seconds, Shades in 16, Brutes in 24. A Brute costs 2 ember if it reaches the door.
